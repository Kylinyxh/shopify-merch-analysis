# Decision Rules

## Required output per SKU

- Primary Decision
- Clearance flag
- Bundle flag
- Price Increase flag
- Reduce Discount flag
- Restock flag
- Increase Exposure flag
- Stop Restock flag
- Reason

## Clearance

Use multiple signals:
- low sales
- low/negative contribution
- high forward inventory
- mature or legacy lifecycle
- season-adjusted weak trend

Seasonal off-season alone is not enough.

## Bundle

Good candidates:
- excess inventory with acceptable economics
- complementary accessories
- low standalone sales but strong product fit
- situations where direct price cutting is undesirable

## Price increase

Test when:
- sales are strong
- contribution margin is below target
- actual discount is already low
- demand is not in decline
- inventory pressure is not extreme

## Reduce discount

Use actual Shopify Discounts, not Compare-at Price.

## Restock

Strong demand + good contribution + low forward coverage.

## Increase exposure

Low sales + high margin + adequate stock, especially if traffic is low.

## Bikes

Do not write “Bikes strategy is no discount”.
Use: “Current selling prices are not markdown-discounted; Compare-at Price is not used as discount evidence.”
